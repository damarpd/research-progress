## Literature review

### Fundamental diagram of urban rail transit considering train-passenger interaction

- A study from [Seo et al, (2023)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11116-022-10281-0#Fn2)
- Novelty: incorporating passengers in analytical way of Fundamental Diagram
- Objective: propose a model of dynamics of urban rail transit considering physical interaction between train-congestion and passenger-congestion.
- **Definition**:
    - train-congestion: congestion involving consecutive trains using the same tracks
    - passenger-congestion: congestion of passengers who are boarding to a train, namely, bottleneck congestion at the doors of a train while it is stopped at a station

<u>Discussion</u>

- This study proposed three scope of modeling of dynamics of urban rail transit $\rightarrow$ microscopic, fundamental diagram, and macroscopic.
